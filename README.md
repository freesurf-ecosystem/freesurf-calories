# FreeSurf Calorie Tracker

Snap a photo of your meal and get instant calorie and macro estimates. No account required.

Take a picture of any meal and the app identifies each food item with estimated calories, protein, carbs, and fat. Track daily and weekly intake with a simple ring chart. All data stays on your device.

## Why it's free

Food analysis runs on a self-hosted vision AI model (Llama 3.2 Vision) on our own GPU infrastructure. No per-photo API fees. The app is supported by minimal, non-intrusive ads.

Subscription removes ads.

## How it works

1. Take a photo of your meal
2. The vision model identifies each food item
3. You get estimated calories, protein, carbs, and fat per item
4. Edit anything that looks off — you know your food better than AI
5. Track daily and weekly intake

## Privacy

- No account or login required
- Photos are processed on our GPU and immediately deleted — nothing stored
- Meal log saved locally on your device only
- No data leaves your phone except the photo for analysis

## Features

- Camera capture or photo library import
- AI food identification with macro estimates
- Manual quick-add for known items
- Edit/delete any item
- Daily calorie ring + weekly bar chart
- Calorie goal setting
- All data stored locally (AsyncStorage)
